# Optional exterior ground-cover visual

Poly Haven `fern_02`, CC0-1.0: https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02
License: https://polyhaven.com/license

Unmodified 1K glTF distribution, 1,146,361 bytes including geometry and three maps.
Exact upstream URLs and SHA-256 hashes are in sources.json. Runtime uses only
`fern_02_b` (1,660 vertices, 2,384 triangles), not the four-variant source scene.
Its source transform is baked into a private geometry, then uniformly fitted
within the pre-existing 1 × 0.7 × 1 metre decorative planting envelope. The 16 median placements stay in their existing bed. The 14 site placements
are constrained outside the canonical Ground footprint and central arrival
band by sitePlantingLayout.ts; their scales are retained. Two old outliers
growing through vehicle paving were removed. Two groups are instanced.

This is a proposed visual planting study, not a canonical botanical inventory
or an assertion that this species is installed/approved for the site. No imported
names, extras, cameras, lights, animations or capabilities enter the Oyi hierarchy.
Ground architectural ingestion is not involved. Model, buffer or image failure
retains the original procedural planting without suspending the Twin.

An earlier shrub_02 candidate was rejected on visual inspection (sparse twigs);
none of that rejected asset is included here.
